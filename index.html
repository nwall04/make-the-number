It is 1985. You are Don Russell, a rising controller with a bonus on the line and earnings to deliver. Your team makes his real decisions, then takes over a broken board.

Join your team

The room code appears on the big screen when class starts. One device per team. A laptop works best, but a phone is fine.

How it works

  1. 1

    Form a team

    Group up and join on one device with the room code from the screen.

  2. 2

    Make Don’s calls

    Five decisions from the case, from Cook & Spector in 1985 to ETI in 1991. You get 90 seconds each to argue and vote.

  3. 3

    Fix the board

    Spend 100 governance points to correct the failures that let ETI’s numbers go wrong.

What you are scored on

Reported earningsWhat the market and your boss see
Bonus and careerMoney, recognition, promotion
Information qualityWhether anyone can still trust the numbers
Legal exposureYour personal risk if it all comes out. Lower is better

Think about which of these actually matters.
