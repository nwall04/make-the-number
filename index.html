Make the
Number

It is 1985. You are Don Russell, a rising controller with a bonus on the line and earnings to deliver. Your team makes his real decisions, then takes over a broken board.

Join your team

The room code appears on the big screen when class starts. One phone or computer per team (a computer is easier).

How it works

  1. 1

    Join with your group

    Use one phone or computer per group. Enter the room code from the screen and your group number.

  2. 2

    Follow Don’s career

    Follow Don through two companies: Cook & Spector, where he is controller (1985–1989), and ETI, where he is CFO (1989–1991). Make his five toughest decisions, with 90 seconds each to discuss and vote. Short teaching slides between rounds connect each decision to Chapter 13: earnings management, boards, audit and compensation committees, and Sarbanes-Oxley.

  3. 3

    Fix the board

    After the twist, your team becomes ETI’s new board. Spend 100 governance points to correct the six failures that let the numbers go wrong.

What you are scored on

Reported earningsWhat the market and your boss see
Bonus and careerMoney, recognition, promotion
Information qualityWhether anyone can still trust the numbers
Legal exposureYour personal risk if it all comes out. Lower is better

Think about which of these actually matters.
